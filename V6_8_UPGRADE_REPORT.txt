NEW IN V6.8
-----------
1. CAPITAL GROWTH CONTROLLER
   Persists a daily equity anchor/peak in SQLite using the configured session timezone.
   Tracks 15% target and 20% stretch objective.

2. PROFIT-PROTECTION PHASES
   build            : normal evidence-based risk logic.
   target_protect   : >=15% daily growth -> 0.50x risk multiplier.
   stretch_lock     : >=20% daily growth -> new entries paused until next local day.
   giveback_protect : after >=8% peak gain, >3 percentage-point giveback -> 0.35x risk.

3. NO TARGET CHASING
   There is deliberately no martingale, loss-recovery multiplier, forced trade frequency,
   risk increase because target is behind, or Luna override of deterministic risk gates.
   Compounding comes from current-equity sizing plus positive expectancy, not risk chasing.

4. LUNA GETS CAPITAL CONTEXT
   The compact pre-trade packet now includes daily growth/peak/target/stretch/phase so Luna
   understands portfolio context, while the system prompt explicitly forbids confirming a
   trade merely to chase the target.

5. CAPITAL STATUS TOOL
   27_CAPITAL_GROWTH_STATUS.bat gives one-screen daily growth/target/protection status.
