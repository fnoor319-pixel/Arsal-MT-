TRADING MACHINE V6.9 - EDGE RECOVERY SCALP LAB (DEMO ONLY)

Problem observed from V6.8 runtime evidence
- Aggregate recent-demo Monte Carlo was negative.
- A large historical score could still dominate recent weak shadow evidence.
- Some catastrophically weak shadow candidates waited too long for normal rejection.
- The approved scalp pool could remain empty for a long time.

What V6.9 changes
1. Recent actual DEMO edge hierarchy
   - strategy -> family -> symbol -> overall portfolio.
   - strategy can be quarantined after repeated bad actual DEMO evidence.
   - weak family/symbol/portfolio evidence reduces risk only; it does not permanently freeze the whole machine.

2. Symbol-level Monte Carlo
   - XAUUSDm, USOILm and BTCUSDm are evaluated separately.
   - one weak symbol no longer has to define the whole machine's edge state.

3. Shadow evidence re-ranking
   - recent shadow mean-R receives much more weight in candidate ranking.
   - strong backtest scores cannot easily hide a badly performing current-market shadow candidate.

4. Early catastrophic shadow quarantine
   - a strategy with >=8 shadow outcomes and mean <= -0.25R is skipped/demoted early.
   - the original normal rejection rule remains as a second path.

5. Strict scalp fast-track
   - scalp only, DEMO only.
   - requires >=20 shadow trades, mean >= +0.18R, PF >=1.40, DD <=2.5R,
     OOS PF >=1.10 and stability >=0.70.
   - this can grow the approved scalp pool faster without simply lowering the normal quality bar.

6. Carefully widened DEMO trial pool
   - candidate cap 20 -> 36.
   - OOS minimum trades 20 -> 12, but OOS PF is raised to >=1.05.
   - rejected/generated strategies are still non-executable.

7. Luna/API protection unchanged
   - edge-recovery filtering happens before the paid Luna call.
   - weak candidates therefore do not consume API credit.
   - no additional Luna calls were added.

8. Risk philosophy unchanged
   - no martingale.
   - no risk increase to chase the 15/20 daily objective.
   - all existing DEMO hard locks, daily-loss, drawdown, spread, Spartan, SuperLearner and Luna gates remain.

New status tool
- 28_EDGE_RECOVERY_STATUS.bat

Important interpretation
The old negative Monte Carlo result is historical evidence and will not disappear immediately after installing this patch. V6.9 changes selection/risk/learning behavior going forward. As new V6.9 trades close, the rolling window will replace old outcomes and show whether the edge is actually improving.
