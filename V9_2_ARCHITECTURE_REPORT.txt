LEARNING PRINCIPLE
Hard safety rules are few. Intelligence layers are score/risk/exit evidence rather
than a return to ten serial vetoes. Strong evidence receives modest preference;
weak evidence is de-risked or raises the score floor within tight bounds.

RECOVERY PRINCIPLE
A losing trade is never rescued by doubling exposure. V9.2 may cut a deteriorating
trade early. A reverse position requires a fresh opposite signal and normal bounded risk.
