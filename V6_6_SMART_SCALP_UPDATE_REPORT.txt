---------------

V6.6 CHANGES
------------
1. Scalp-aware SuperLearner expectancy gate
   - Uses reward:risk and expected-R for scalp decisions instead of relying on one high fixed P(win) cutoff.
   - Blends model probability with adaptive, collective and session evidence.
   - Safety/risk sizing still occurs deterministically after approval.

2. Cleaner learning memory
   - backtest_rejected and shadow_rejected research outcomes no longer poison execution memory.
   - historical_validated and shadow_approved shadow outcomes receive reduced research weights.
   - one-time V6.6 execution-memory rebuild is wired into the DEMO engine.

3. Spartan confluence normalization
   - Optional unavailable news/DOM evidence is treated as unknown, not as an automatic failed vote.
   - Confluence is normalized against evidence actually available.
   - Gold/Oil hard risk/session/market-quality gates remain.

4. Compact GPT/Luna final reviewer
   - One compact dynamic market packet is sent per materially new candidate.
   - No conversation history is sent.
   - Structured reply is intentionally short: confirm/hold, confidence, reason code, short reason.
   - GPT remains a final reviewer only; it does not directly change lot size, SL/TP or safety limits.
   - GPT context is now available for Gold, Oil and Crypto candidates that reach the final review stage.
   - Existing material-state cache prevents repeated paid reviews of unchanged setups.

5. Compact post-trade GPT learning
   - Sends a small post-trade evidence packet.
   - Produces a short diagnostic/hypothesis for journaling; it cannot autonomously rewrite live parameters.

6. New-bar entry evaluation
   - Entry intelligence can evaluate once per newly closed M1 bar instead of repeatedly logging the same candle.
   - Position/trailing management remains independent.

7. Diagnostics
   - New 22_SCALP_HEALTH.bat / scalp_health.py reports strategy inventory, decision funnel, SuperLearner and GPT reach.
