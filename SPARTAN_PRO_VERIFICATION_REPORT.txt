ARCHITECTURE NOTE
machine_supervisor.py is intentionally a process supervisor, not the trading brain.
The actual decision/execution brain is trading_machine.py + spartan_pro.py + spartan_llm.py.
Moving GPT/order-flow logic into machine_supervisor.py would duplicate responsibilities and make the system less reliable.

KNOWN EXTERNAL DEPENDENCY
