V6.0 SCALP INTELLIGENCE UPDATE REPORT
Build: 2026-08-20

ROOT CAUSE FIXED
----------------
Previous selection could fill the approved candidate list with high-scoring trend strategies before scalp candidates were considered. Even when historical super-scalp candidates existed, the live executor could remain trend-heavy. V6 fixes routing: approved scalp -> eligible trial scalp -> approved general -> trial general.

SCALP RESEARCH ENGINE
---------------------
1. Expanded scalp families:
   super_scalp, scalp, micro_momentum, pullback_scalp, breakout_scalp, mean_revert_scalp.
2. Regime-specific scalp-dominant generation weights.
3. Live regime seeding also consumes collective + session memory.
4. Evolution parent ranking prefers scalp candidates under scalp-primary mode.
5. Two-stage backtesting for scalps:
   - latest-window fast prescreen
   - full history + OOS + walk-forward only if worth the compute
6. Metrics added: expectancy-R, avg/median hold bars, trades/1000 bars.
7. Scalp validation includes max holding-time discipline and scalp-specific PF/OOS gates.

CONTEXT / 'SENTIMENT'
---------------------
- Market session tags: Asia, London, London/NY overlap, New York, off-hours; crypto-specific session tags.
- Session-family memory is persistent per symbol/family/regime/session/direction.
- Internal market sentiment proxy combines momentum/body/volume/DOM context.
- SuperLearner can raise threshold when sentiment/session evidence conflicts and modestly lower it when strong scalp-session evidence supports the signal.
- External news/social sentiment is intentionally not fabricated.
