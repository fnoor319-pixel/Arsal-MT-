KEY V7 DIFFERENCE
-----------------
The old execution path mostly waited for exact strategy row_signal conditions on a newly
closed M1 candle. V7 adds a specialist execution brain that recognizes market structures
and can arm a setup, then react to a live-tick continuation inside the next minute.

The hunter does NOT invent an executable strategy. It borrows only an existing qualified
scalp strategy from the shadow-approved / historical-validated DEMO trial pool. This keeps
the research/evidence chain connected to execution.

MICRO PLAYBOOKS
---------------
Momentum Burst:
  strong body + volume + ADX/momentum + trend alignment.

Impulse/Pullback/Resume:
  3-candle sequence: impulse -> controlled pullback -> resumption.

Squeeze Breakout:
  compressed recent ranges -> volatility expansion -> structural break.

Wick Rejection:
  liquidity-extreme rejection with wick/close-position/RSI evidence.

Exhaustion Snapback:
  extension in one direction followed by rejection/reversal evidence.

RISK
----
V7 does not add martingale, loss-doubling or target-chasing risk.
A micro-hunter trade has its own soft risk multiplier <= 1.0x, and all older hard ceilings
remain authoritative. The 15% / 20% daily growth values remain objectives/protection phases,
not a reason to force a trade or increase risk.

LUNA
----
Luna sees the micro playbook context plus the existing quant/market/learning context.
It still cannot set lot size, SL/TP, daily limits or bypass deterministic gates.
